MGSKJ_upload

MGSKJ_upload is a private desktop tool run by the operator of a single YouTube channel. It uploads that channel's own finished videos through the YouTube Data API, schedules their publish time, and lets the operator check, edit or remove the channel's own videos.

It is not a public service. There is no sign-up, no other users, and no data is collected from viewers or from anyone other than the channel operator.

What the app does

  • Signs in with Google OAuth, approved by the channel owner.
  • Reads the channel's own ID, title and uploaded videos (to confirm the right channel, avoid duplicates and show scheduled videos).
  • Uploads videos and thumbnails prepared by the operator, as private or scheduled.
  • At the operator's request, changes a video's privacy, publish time or details, or deletes the operator's own video.
